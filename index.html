我们做什么 / What we do

站外英文能力页

我们将产品和产能整理为便于买家及AI检索、理解的英文页面；内容以工厂确认资料为准，第三方检索及引用结果不作保证。先被看见，才有资格谈生意。

We organize your products and capacity into an English page that is easier for buyers and their AI to find and understand; content follows what the factory confirms, and third-party search or citation results are not guaranteed. Being seen is the precondition for doing business.

在约定服务范围内跟进询盘 / Within the agreed scope, we follow up on inquiries

找上门的买家，是您已经花了成本换来的；中途断掉的询盘，等于把到手的生意亲手送给同行。找上门的询盘，我们在约定渠道与服务期内跟进并维护台账。

A buyer who came to you already cost you money to win; a conversation that goes quiet hands that business to a competitor. For inquiries that arrive, we follow up within the agreed channels and service period and keep a ledger.

怎么做 / How it works

  1. 先看样品我们先用贵司公开的信息做一页英文样品；公开资料里口径不一致的地方，也在样品里帮您理顺改准，您只看一眼、确认一下哪个数字为准就行。
    Sample first: we build a one-page English sample from your public information and straighten inconsistent figures in it — you only confirm which number is correct.
  2. 两周小试点样品您先看，觉得值再做。您不用填表、不用学新东西，剩下的跟进我们当陪跑全程跑完。
    Two-week pilot: you see the sample first and proceed only if it is worth it. No forms, no new tools to learn — we run the follow-up as your companion throughout.
  3. 约定维护期内持续服务During the agreed maintenance period, we keep the page normally accessible, work on search visibility, follow up inquiries within the agreed channels and scope, and maintain a ledger. Search indexing and ranking, whether a reply is completed, and reply timing are not guaranteed.
    Ongoing service during the agreed maintenance period

做完后您自己就能查到结果 / Check the result yourself

  • Google 上搜贵司名字和产品词，看页面出不出来。Search Google for your company name and product terms, and see whether the page comes up.
