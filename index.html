我们做什么 / What we do

站外英文能力页

海外买家搜不到您，您就不在他们的供应商名单里，平台之外的订单从一开始就与您无关。我们把您的产品和产能做成买家和他们 AI 在平台外能搜到、读得准的英文页面——先被看见，才有资格谈生意。

If overseas buyers cannot find you, you are not on their supplier list at all — orders outside the platforms never even reach you. We turn your products and capacity into an English page that buyers and their AI can find and read accurately outside the platforms. Being seen is the precondition for doing business.

询盘跟到底

找上门的买家，是您已经花了成本换来的；中途断掉的询盘，等于把到手的生意亲手送给同行。我们把阿里、中国制造网、邮件、WhatsApp 各个渠道的询盘都跟到底，并做 90 天丢单审计，把断掉的线再接回来。

A buyer who came to you already cost you money to win; a conversation that goes quiet hands that business to a competitor. We follow inquiries across Alibaba, Made-in-China, email and WhatsApp to the end, and audit 90 days of lost inquiries to reconnect the ones that went cold.

怎么做 / How it works

  1. 先看样品我们先用贵司公开的信息做一页英文样品；公开资料里口径不一致的地方，也在样品里帮您理顺改准，您只看一眼、确认一下哪个数字为准就行。
    Sample first: we build a one-page English sample from your public information and straighten inconsistent figures in it — you only confirm which number is correct.
  2. 两周小试点样品您先看，觉得值再做。您不用填表、不用学新东西，剩下的跟进我们当陪跑全程跑完。
    Two-week pilot: you see the sample first and proceed only if it is worth it. No forms, no new tools to learn — we run the follow-up as your companion throughout.
  3. 之后一直生效页面一直能被搜到，每条询盘都有回复、有台账，不再出现问一句就没下文的情况。
    Then it keeps working: the page stays searchable, and every inquiry gets a reply and a ledger entry.

做完后您自己就能查到结果 / Check the result yourself

  • Google 上搜贵司名字和产品词，看页面出不出来。Search Google for your company name and product terms, and see whether the page comes up.
  • 问一下买家 AI，看它报的数字准不准。Ask a buyer-side AI about your products, and see whether the numbers it reports are right.
  • 询盘的台账我也会给您，每条回没回、谈到哪一步，上面都有。Read the inquiry ledger: every inquiry, whether it was replied to, and how far each conversation went.
