About us

ZHIDA GROUP is a manufacturer and exporter of home furniture based in Longjiang Town, Shunde District, Foshan — China's furniture manufacturing hub. The group owns ZHIDA FURNITURE, ZHIDA TEXTILES and ZHIDA STEEL PIPE&TUBE, and sells under the brands ECO, RONAMDE and MACCHIATO. Its factory covers 20,000 square metres in Longjiang, and its showroom has 4 floors covering 15,000 square metres. Export experience: 20 years (per platform pages).
