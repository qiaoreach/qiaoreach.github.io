Foshan Zhongsen Furniture Co., Ltd. (TRINITY)

Hotel Furniture Packages for Star-Rated Hotels, Villas and Apartments, Longjiang, Shunde, Foshan, Guangdong, China

About

Zhongsen, whose predecessor brand is TRINITY, is a hotel furniture manufacturer in Longjiang, Shunde, Foshan, specialising in entire hotel furniture packages for star-rated hotels, villas and apartments, supplying contract furniture to hotel groups, independent hotels and franchised hotel brands. (source)

What they make

  • Hotel guestroom / bedroom furniture packages
  • Hotel lobby and reception sofas
  • Hotel lounge sofas and armchairs
  • Resort furniture (solid wood / teak)
  • Restaurant and bar sofas
